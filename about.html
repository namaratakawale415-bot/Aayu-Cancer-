The Ayurvedic Lens on Cancer

Our Philosophy of Cancer Care

In Ayurveda, cancer — referred to in classical texts as Arbuda or Granthi — is understood as a deep systemic imbalance, not merely a localized disease. Our interventions address the root cause, not just the symptoms.

⚖️

Dosha Imbalance

Cancer arises from deep imbalances in the three fundamental bio-energies — Vata, Pitta, and Kapha. Every treatment is tailored to the patient's unique constitutional imbalance.

🔥

Weakened Agni

A weakened digestive fire (Agni) leads to Ama (toxin) accumulation in the body's channels, disrupting healthy tissue formation. We restore this vital fire through targeted therapies.

🌱

Samprapti Bhanga

Our care focuses on breaking the disease pathway — reversing the pathological binding of doshas and tissues — through Rasayana, Panchakarma, diet, and herbal medicines.

🤝

Integrative, Not Alternative

Aayu Cancer Care works alongside your oncology team — not in place of it. Our Ayurvedic protocols complement chemotherapy, radiation, and surgery by managing side effects, rebuilding immunity, and improving treatment tolerance. This collaborative model ensures the best of both worlds for every patient.

What We Offer

Our Core Services

Every service is customized to the patient's unique constitution (Prakriti), cancer type, and stage of their journey — physical, emotional, and spiritual.

💊

Pain Management

Using exclusive herbal and herbo-mineral formulations (Shallaki, Guggulu, Haridra/Turmeric, Nirgundi) alongside Abhyanga (medicated oil massage) and Potli therapy to address cancer-related pain by unblocking the body's channels (Srotas).

🌿

Rasayana Therapy

The cornerstone of integrative oncology at Aayu — systemic rejuvenation using Ashwagandha, Guduchi, Shatavari, and Chyavanprash to modulate immunity, protect healthy cells during chemotherapy, and rebuild vitality from within.

🛁

Panchakarma (Detoxification)

Carefully adapted purification procedures — including Basti (medicated enema) and Marma therapy — to gently eliminate accumulated toxins, restore digestive fire, and relieve the side effects of conventional cancer treatment.

🍽️

Diet & Lifestyle Guidance

Personalized plans based on the patient's constitution — emphasizing warm, freshly cooked, easily digestible foods to strengthen Agni, reduce Ama, and support recovery. Includes daily routine (Dinacharya) and stress reduction guidance.

🧘

Psycho-Spiritual Support

Sattvavajaya Chikitsa (Ayurvedic psychological therapy), Pranayama, guided meditation, and mind-supporting herbs like Brahmi to manage anxiety, depression, and existential distress at every stage of the cancer journey.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family & Caregiver Support

Comprehensive support for family members and caregivers — including emotional counseling, grief literacy, bereavement support, and practical guidance — extending our care beyond the patient to everyone who loves them.

A Common Misconception

Palliative Care Is Not
Only End-of-Life Care

Palliative care is a specialized medical approach dedicated to improving quality of life at any stage of cancer — not just at the end. It can begin at the moment of diagnosis and run alongside any curative treatment.

Our Ayurvedic palliative approach addresses the whole person — managing physical symptoms, emotional distress, and spiritual suffering — while actively helping patients tolerate their conventional treatment better.

Research evidence: Studies in the New England Journal of Medicine have shown that patients receiving early palliative care may experience not only improved quality of life — but also prolonged survival.

We support patients at every stage:
At Diagnosis — Managing the shock, fear, and initial symptoms with calm, holistic support
During Treatment — Reducing side effects of chemo, radiation, and surgery; protecting healthy tissue
Survivorship — Rebuilding strength, immunity, and confidence after treatment concludes
Advanced Illness — Providing comfort, dignity, and relief when curative options are limited
For Families — Emotional, spiritual, and bereavement support that continues beyond the patient's life
