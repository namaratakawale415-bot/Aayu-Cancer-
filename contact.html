Frequently Asked

Common Questions

Do I need a prior appointment?

Yes, all consultations are by appointment. You can book via this form, by phone, or through WhatsApp. Walk-in slots may be available at NIRAMAY on select days.

Can Ayurvedic care be taken alongside chemotherapy?

Absolutely. Dr. Kate specializes in integrative palliative care — designed specifically to complement and support conventional oncology treatments, not replace them.

Is online/teleconsultation available?

Teleconsultations are available for follow-up visits and patients from outside Nagpur. Please mention your preference in the message form above.

What documents should I bring?

Please bring recent diagnostic reports (pathology, imaging, biopsy), a list of current medications, and any prior treatment summaries to your first consultation.
